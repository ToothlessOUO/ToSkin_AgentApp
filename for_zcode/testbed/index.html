Repaint the wake from its samples so it can reach the sheet colour.
The wake is re-drawn from the sample list every frame instead of being accumulated into the bitmap and faded. A cell takes the level of the freshest sample over it, and once that sample is older than its life the cell is simply not painted — the sheet shows through exactly.
javascript
const draw = () => {
  context.clearRect(0, 0, rect.width, rect.height);
  /* every cell once, at the freshest sample's level */
  paintCells(countingSortByLevel());
};
Sweep the pointer across the sheet — the lens head stays under the cursor and the dotted wake ages out behind it, reaching the sheet colour exactly at the end of its life.
